## 2026-10-24 Kitchen in Shop: at home + shopping cart, first simple version (Emmanuel, via the Maya handoff)
- Intent (handoff HANDOFF-shop-kitchen.md on branch sz-filters-20261024): a personal kitchen stock "like a wardrobe for food" plus a shopping cart for products to buy later, both inside Shop under Fuel. The detailed stock and cart interactions were NOT specified; this is a deliberately small first version.
- Shipped: a third Shop tab **Kitchen** (store-picks.js kitHTML, kitchen-core.js pure logic, styles at the end of store-picks.css). **At home** = lowispike.kitchen.v1 (private; lowispike.* keys travel with the account through account-sync-core.js). **Shopping cart** = the existing shopping list (LOWI_SHELF / SH.list), never a second list; new LOWI_SHELF.dropList(id). Rows: cart "Bought" moves the item off the list into At home; x removes from the cart; x in At home takes it out and offers "+ Buy again" once (nothing is ever added by itself); items ticked off in the shop scan can be moved in one tap. "+ Shopping cart" on a Supermarket product card. Same see-through glass as the rest of Shop (the world shows behind it, still, like every Shop tab since the Oct 18 crash fix). The kitchen list is data-noi18n so the page translator never renames what people typed.
- Not done on purpose: the Seller Zone Category/Region rows stay on sz-filters-20261024 (not merged here); the separate Seller Zone "Snacks to buy" list was left as it is.
- Open design questions (proposals, not built): amounts and units per item; best-before dates and "use soon"; grouping (fridge, freezer, pantry) or by food group; using what's at home in Cook suggestions; scanning a receipt or barcode straight into the kitchen; merging "Snacks to buy" into the cart; a shared household kitchen (consent per member); removal sync across two devices editing at the same time (the account merge joins lists by id, so an item taken out on one device can come back if the other device changed the list too).
- Tests: tests/kitchen.test.cjs. Browser check: tools/check-kitchen.cjs <w> <h> <root|url> <out> <en|de|es> [day].
