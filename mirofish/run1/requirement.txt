Simulate how the public reacts when LOWiSPIKE, exactly as described in the uploaded walkthrough of the live beta, is shared on social media as a free beta. The people react to that real experience (the screens, words, limits and friction in the walkthrough), not to a sales pitch. The simulated people are exactly the twelve members of the beta reader panel in section 10 of the walkthrough (eight English speakers, one German speaker from Hamburg, one Colombian Spanish speaker from Medellín, one Swiss German speaker from Winterthur and one Japanese speaker from Osaka), each posting in their own language. Do not simulate the founder, the app or any company. Predict: who loves it and why, who dislikes it and why, what confuses people in the first minutes, what would make them stop using it after two weeks, what they would change or add first, which health or privacy concerns come up. The founder has not set a price for the planned LOWiSPIKE Plus subscription: let the people work out what Plus should include and what monthly price they consider ideal, and at what price it becomes too expensive, and report the range and the most common ideal price. Point out the single most important change the founder should make before a wider launch.
