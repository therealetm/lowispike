# LOWiSPIKE: what a new person actually experiences (written walkthrough of the live beta)

This is a step-by-step script of the real app as it is on 26 October 2026 (live build 20261026-071738), written from the app's own code and on-screen English text, not from a sales pitch. Quoted lines in "double quotes" are the exact words the app shows. Things that are planned but not built are marked PLANNED. Nothing here is medical advice, and the app itself makes no medical claims.

LOWiSPIKE is a free web app (a PWA) at therealetm.github.io/lowispike. It is not in the App Store yet. It runs in English, German and Spanish. One founder in Flensburg, Germany, builds it with AI coding agents that ship small fixes many times a day. Most testing so far is the founder on his own iPhone plus automated tests. It is a beta.

## 1. First open on a phone

On a phone browser the first thing is a guide to add the app to the Home Screen, with one line on top: "Free app: eat smarter and become your healthiest self, as a game."

Then the first real screen. Small heading "FOOD & HABIT TRACKER". Headline: "Eat smarter. Become your healthiest self, as a game." Under it: "Every healthy habit you track powers up your companion:" with five chips: "Shop & scan", "Fuel (meals)", "Water", "Move", "Gap (fasting)". A founder line: "Made by Eman, who lost 45 kg in 7 months." A live, animated glass companion floats on the screen (drawn live, no stock pictures). Buttons: "Get started" and "I already have an account".

## 2. Name and first reward

"YOUR NAME". The companion says: "Hi! I'm your companion. What's your first name?" Field "Your first name", button "Continue". If it is empty: "Type your first name first."

Next: "YOUR FIRST XP". "{name}, you earned your first spark! Create a free account to keep it." A chip shows "+5 XP · first spark". Button "Create free account", small print "Free. Takes 20 seconds."

## 3. Account and setup

The account form is email and password ("Two fields. That's it."). Then four setup steps:

1. Birthday (and optionally birth time). The birthday gives the companion its own face, colours and name in a reveal. Before this step the companion has no name. The standard companion is called Misty; with a birthday each person gets their own.
2. Optional profile photo.
3. Goals: what you want most.
4. Grocery shopping: how often you shop. Bubbles: Once / Twice / 3 times / More per week, or It varies. Optional weekdays. "Later" skips. This sets how long the worlds glow after a shopping trip.

About you (setup and Profile) asks gender with four choices: Woman / Man / Non-binary / Prefer not to say, and optional health conditions as chips plus one free line "Something else? Type it here". Health data stays on the phone: no account backup, no Community, no server.

Then an optional starter plan: "STARTER PLAN". "{companion} is ready for your first adventure." "Up to 4 quick questions so your first steps fit your day. Every one is optional. No health questions." Buttons "Start" and "Not now". After the answers: "Your next steps" – "They sit on Today. Each one opens the part of the app that does it." Skipping says: "Any time: Profile › About you › Starter plan."

New accounts start at full energy for their first day.

## 4. Home: the companion and the bubbles around her

Home is one screen: the companion in the middle and round glass bubbles around her. A short guided tour (replayable in Settings > Tutorial; every card has a speaker that reads it aloud) says, card by card:

- "Tell me your birthday and I become your very own companion."
- "Hold me and my card opens: my energy, my level and what I still need."
- "Tap Gap to see how long it has been since your last meal."
- "Tap Fuel to log a meal, find ideas in Cook or check products in Shop."
- "Tap Water and pick what you drank: 10 sips, a glass or a bottle."
- "Tap Move in the evening and pick Casual, Steady or Epic for your steps." (Casual is under 5k steps, Steady 5k to 10k, Epic 10k plus. LOWiSPIKE does not count steps itself; you read your phone's step counter and pick.)
- "Tap Sleep once a day and tell me how long you slept."
- "Tap Scan and point the camera at your food: I tell you how much it spikes."
- "Tap Community to meet everyone else and their companions."
- "Hold Community for your profile, day or night, language and your account."
- "Drag the joystick and I fly, or tap it three times for autopilot."
- Last card: "Start small: a glass of water, then your next meal."

Holding Scan opens every bubble at once; a quick tap opens Scan.

The companion's energy comes from food (40%), water (30%) and movement (30%). It shows in her face, how fast she flies and how bright her world glows. Nothing ever goes negative and spiky meals simply earn nothing: no punishment, no red numbers.

Flying: drag the joystick to fly, hold = forward, 2 taps = back, 3 taps = autopilot. Zooming out shows a galaxy of everyone's companions joined by threads of light. She can dive into body-organ "cell worlds" where guide characters teach body science and quiz you later.

## 5. Scan (products at the shop)

Scan has a live barcode camera, a typed barcode, a barcode from a photo and a typed product search ("Or find a product…"). The product card gives one verdict: "Lower impact", "Small rise" or "Spike likely", or "Skip it" with a reason and about how many grams of carbs, for example "About {net} g net carbs." An unknown product asks for a "Photo of the pack". Results are estimates from the label, never a promise of "no spike". A new product you are first to find is "caught" with a stamp and a small reward (a collection, like a Pokédex). After a check you can Add to bucket or Skip; the day's bucket lists items, spike points and prices (typed or real reports, never invented), and "Today's groceries + what to cook" suggests up to 3 recipes that use them.

Shop has tabs: Supermarket, Seller Zone (real snacks you can order: "Strictly checked, with photo and price. Shops near you first."), Kitchen ("Keep track of what you have at home, plus a shopping cart for later. Tap Bought and it moves into your kitchen.") and Give a hand ("Pay for someone's healthy groceries, or share your own basket.").

## 6. Fuel: logging meals and the Gap

Fuel has Shop, Cook and Log meal with breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack slots and nutrition against daily targets. You can type a meal (unlimited) or snap a photo: an AI estimates the dishes, portion, calories, protein, carbs, sugar, fiber and fat. Three free photo checks per person per day. Estimates can be wrong and are labelled as estimates. The same food only earns once; meals have an XP cap.

Cook: choose Cook/Bake or Buy, then Meals or Sweet snacks, then type a dish or Shuffle (a reel that settles on a lower-spike version of a meal without replacing what the dish is). Also Swipe foods, All recipes, My Recipe Book, By ingredient, and a LOWiSPIKE GPT link for a deeper look (manual, nothing comes back into the app automatically).

Gap shows the time since your last meal (fasting). It also has "what your blood sugar typically does after a meal, phase by phase, and easy ways to lower the spike", with a speaker on each card. An after-meal checklist pays XP only for finished actions (with a timer and a confirm).

## 7. Companion growth and rewards

Only real actions give XP (a meal about 15, a move up to 30, daily quests). An active day is about 80 to 150 XP. There are 25 levels and ten companion forms, from a glowing cell to a celestial organism: Spark, Bud, Sprite, Drifter, Guardian, Radiant, Nova, Titan, Monarch, Majesty. The first two evolutions come in the first days; about a week for the fourth form, about two weeks for the fifth, about a month for the sixth. Locked forms show only a mystery orb, "???" and the XP needed, so each evolution is a reveal with a short animation. Aura colours and renaming are unlocked along the way. Signed in on two devices, the companion, level and logs are the same everywhere.

Move has short sessions including in-bed, seated and walking options, and a Mirror mode that uses the camera: "Stand sideways to your phone. The glowing outline shows the move and counts every clean rep." A completed session, not just opening it, cares for the companion.

## 8. Community

Community has Chat (a sci-fi style chat room with replies, mentions, reactions and member profiles), For You (a feed of posts and meal photos; you review the AI estimate before posting a meal photo, up to three photos a day) and a weekly Ranking plus a Care board that ranks daily care (meals, water, moves, scans). People lists everyone in the app with name and photo. "The Ranking and Add a friend now show only real people. The bots stay in the chat with their daily photos." So the chat contains clearly marked demo/bot members next to real people. Meal photos stay on the phone unless you share one.

Known in the beta: the founder's own iPhone has had a crash when opening Community and then the chat; fixes are being worked on.

## 9. What it costs

Today everything is free. Nobody can pay for anything. PLANNED: a paid "LOWiSPIKE Plus" subscription, sold through a payment provider on the website. What Plus includes and what it costs are NOT decided yet. There is no price, so the founder wants to learn from people what Plus should include and what monthly price feels right to them. Also PLANNED: affiliate links to products and grocery stores.
