Support

Questions, problems or a bug? Write to therealetm@gmail.com. If the app crashed, open Profile > About & info, tap Copy under the crash log and paste it into your email.

Not medical advice

LOWiSPIKE gives general food guidance based on estimates. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any illness and does not replace a doctor. If you have diabetes, use insulin or take medicines, follow your care team.

What we store and why

  • Your account: email address and password (stored securely by our login provider), so you can sign in on any phone.
  • Your profile: name, photo, birthday (day and month show on your Community card, never the year), optional birth time and personality type, your goals and food preferences.
  • What you log: meals, meal photos, scans, streaks, XP and your companion's progress.
  • Community: posts, chat messages, voice messages, photos, reactions and gifts you send. Other members see what you post in Community.
  • On your phone only: settings, language, the crash log and most app state are kept in the app's local storage on your device.

We use this data only to run the app for you (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

Services we use

  • Supabase (database, login, file storage), servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). This is where your account, profile, logs and Community content are stored.
  • Google Gemini (AI food check). Only if you tap Allow: the meal photo you take or pick, or the food name you type, is sent through our server to Google to recognise the food. No name, email or health data is sent with it. You can turn this off any time in Profile > About & info. Without it, you can still type and log every meal. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Open Food Facts (product data and pictures): when you scan or search a product, its barcode or name is looked up there.
  • Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap (weather and nearby shops): only when you use these features and allow location, your approximate position is sent to get the weather or shops near you. It is not stored on our servers.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website version of LOWiSPIKE and may log technical access data such as IP addresses.

Camera, photos, microphone, location

The app asks before it uses any of them. The camera and photos are for meal photos, barcode scans and your profile picture; the microphone for voice messages and dictation; location for weather and nearby shops. You can change these in your iPhone's Settings at any time.

How long we keep data

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account and the data linked to it are deleted.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile > Delete account. This deletes your account and its data. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, limit or object to its use, take it with you, and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to therealetm@gmail.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority.

Age

LOWiSPIKE is for people aged 16 and over.
