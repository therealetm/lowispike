Support

Not medical advice

LOWiSPIKE gives general food guidance based on estimates. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any illness and does not replace a doctor. If you have diabetes, use insulin or take medicines, follow your care team.

What we store and why

  • Your account: email address and password (stored securely by our login provider), so you can sign in on any phone.
  • Your profile: name, photo, birthday (day and month show on your Community card, never the year), optional birth time and personality type, your goals and food preferences.
  • What you log: meals, meal photos, scans, streaks, XP and your companion's progress.
  • Community: posts, chat messages, voice messages, photos, reactions and gifts you send. Other members see what you post in Community.
  • Last online: when you last had the app open. Other members see it as “last online” in the People list.
  • App reviews: if you write one, your stars, text or voice note, its transcript, your name and profile picture are shown on the LOWiSPIKE website.
  • Chats with your companion: kept on your phone only. Each question is sent to the AI to be answered (see Google Gemini).
  • On your phone only: health details (what you pick under “Anything we should look out for?”, such as PCOS, insulin resistance or pre-diabetes, your cycle, weight and blood pressure), settings, language, the crash log and most app state are kept in the app's local storage on your device. They are not part of the account backup. Your weight shows on your Community card only if you turn that on.

We use this data only to run the app for you (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

Services we use

  • Supabase (database, login, file storage), servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). This is where your account, profile, logs and Community content are stored.
  • Google Gemini (AI food check). Only if you tap Allow: the meal photo you take or pick, or the food name you type, is sent through our server to Google to recognise the food. No name, email or health data is sent with it. You can turn this off any time in Profile > About & info. Without it, you can still type and log every meal. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Open Food Facts (product data and pictures): when you scan or search a product, its barcode or name is looked up there.
  • Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap (weather and nearby shops): only when you use these features and allow location, your approximate position is sent to get the weather or shops near you. It is not stored on our servers.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA) hosts the website version of LOWiSPIKE and may log technical access data such as IP addresses (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Google Gemini, more: the same AI (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) also answers your questions to your companion (Talk to Misty, Deep analyze), corrects a meal when you tap “Wrong? Tell me”, translates chat messages, turns voice notes into text and checks shared links. Only the text, photo or audio needed for that one request is sent, through our server, without your name or email. Google may process it outside the EU (USA); this is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU standard contractual clauses. We keep a daily count of AI requests per person for 14 days to stay within limits.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare Inc., USA): lowispike.com runs through Cloudflare for delivery and protection against attacks. Cloudflare processes your IP address and technical connection data for this (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; EU-US Data Privacy Framework).
  • Push notifications: only if you turn them on. Your phone's push address is stored on our server, and messages are delivered through your phone maker's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla).

Legal bases

Running the app, your account and the features you start yourself (for example asking your companion, translating, voice to text): contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The AI food check, location, push notifications and app reviews: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. Security, protection against misuse and technical delivery of the website: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Health details you enter stay on your phone and are not sent to us.

Cookies and storage on your device

We use no tracking or advertising cookies. The app keeps your settings, sign-in and progress in your phone's local storage because the app cannot work without it (§ 25(2) TDDDG). That is why there is no cookie banner.

No automated decisions

No decision with legal or similarly significant effect on you is made automatically (Art. 22 GDPR). The AI's food estimates are suggestions you can always change.

Camera, photos, microphone, location

The app asks before it uses any of them. The camera and photos are for meal photos, barcode scans and your profile picture; the microphone for voice messages and dictation; location for weather and nearby shops. You can change these in your iPhone's Settings at any time.

How long we keep data

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account and the data linked to it are deleted. AI request counts are deleted after 14 days. A review stays on the website until you delete it or ask us to. Data we must keep by law (for example tax records) is kept only as long as the law requires.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile > Delete account. This deletes your account and its data. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, limit or object to its use, take it with you, and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to therealetm@gmail.com. Where we rely on legitimate interest, you can object at any time for reasons that come from your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one in the German federal state where you live or where we are based (Art. 77 GDPR).
